A few common questions.

Who is Ryan Dils?

Ryan Dils is an event producer, DJ, and software engineer from Buffalo, NY. He co-founded HONEY (Honey Group Productions, LLC), the event crew behind 50+ house and EDM parties in Buffalo with more than 10,000 attendees, has logged over 2,000 paid hours as a live DJ, and holds a computer science degree from the University at Buffalo.

What is HONEY (HONEY BFLO)?

HONEY — also known as HONEY BFLO — is an event production group in Buffalo, NY that throws house and EDM parties: golden-hour sunset sets, 360° DJ rigs, club nights, and New Year's Eve shows. Tickets and upcoming events are listed at honeybflo.com.

How do I buy tickets to a HONEY event?

All upcoming HONEY events and tickets are at honeybflo.com. Follow @honeybflo on Instagram and TikTok for announcements and drops.

Does Ryan Dils do consulting?

Yes. Through his firm, Sandra Holdings, LLC, Ryan consults on software projects — full-stack web, automation, and AI integrations — and on events and marketing: production, ticketing, and nightlife brand-building. Email ryandils@ryandils.com to start a conversation.

How can I book Ryan Dils as a DJ?

For DJ bookings, event partnerships, or press, email ryandils@ryandils.com. For HONEY-specific inquiries, contact the crew at info@honeybflo.com.
